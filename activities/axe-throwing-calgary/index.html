Axe Throwing in Calgary

Throw sharp things at targets and feel immediately powerful.

Groups Welcome No Experience Needed Active

Axe throwing has become one of Calgary's favourite group outings, and it's not hard to see why. There's something deeply satisfying about landing a solid throw, and you don't need any experience to have a blast. Most venues have coaches on hand to get you throwing confidently within the first few minutes, and the competitive format keeps things fun all night. Calgary has several dedicated axe throwing venues spread across the city, and the vibe at all of them leans more backyard fire pit than intense sports bar. The Inglewood and Forest Lawn areas have a few spots worth knowing about, and many venues have expanded to accommodate the serious demand from corporate groups and birthday crews who've made this their go-to booking. What keeps people coming back is the genuine progression. Your first throw might bounce off the wall, but within half an hour you're landing sticks and trash-talking your friends. The learning curve is steep in the best way possible, and the moment you land a bullseye for the first time is one of those small, pure wins that sticks with you.

What It's Like

What to Expect

🪓

Coaching from the start

A trained coach walks you through grip, stance, and release before you ever throw. Most people land their first bullseye within 15 minutes.

🎯

Lane-style setup

You'll throw at a wooden target from a set distance. Groups share lanes so there's plenty of cheering between throws.

🏆

Built-in tournaments

Many venues run mini-tournaments during your session so there's always something on the line. Winner gets serious bragging rights.

🍺

Food and drinks available

Most Calgary axe throwing spots have a bar or snack options nearby. Throwing axes and having a cold drink after? Yes.

Insider Advice

Tips for Your First Axe Throwing Event

  • 1 Wear closed-toe shoes. It's a safety requirement at every venue.
  • 2 Don't overthink your release. A relaxed, smooth throw beats a tense, forced one every time.
  • 3 Listen to your coach even if you've thrown before. Each venue has slightly different targets and distances.
  • 4 Make it a competition. Setting stakes (even just bragging rights) makes every throw more exciting.
  • 5 Mixler sometimes organizes group axe throwing outings in Calgary. Join the waitlist to be first to know.
  • 6 Go mid-week if you can. Weekend sessions sell out fast and a quieter weeknight slot means more coaching time per person.
Common Questions

FAQ

Do I need experience to try axe throwing? +
None at all. Coaches teach you everything from scratch, and most people are throwing confidently within their first few turns.
Is axe throwing safe? +
Yes, when done in a supervised venue. All sessions include safety briefings, coaches on the floor, and proper lane barriers.
What should I wear? +
Closed-toe shoes are mandatory. Comfortable, casual clothes work best. Avoid anything too loose around the arms.
How long is a typical session? +
Most sessions run 60 to 90 minutes, which is plenty of time to get coached, practice, and run a tournament with your group.
Can I book axe throwing for a large group? +
Yes. Most Calgary venues can accommodate large groups by booking multiple lanes. Corporate bookings are common and many venues offer packages with food and drinks included.
What is the minimum age for axe throwing? +
Most Calgary venues require participants to be 18 or 19 and up, with valid ID. Check the specific venue's policy when booking.
